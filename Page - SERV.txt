[b]Servitors[/b] is a mod focused on adding Servitors to your RimWorld. Servitors are cybernetically augmented thralls created through the lobotomization and mechanical conversion of humans, repurposed into obedient tools for labour, maintenance, warfare, and countless other functions across the Imperium of Man.

Finally you can convert pawns or prisoners into functional [b]Servitors[/b], with modified stats and needs. Upon completing a surgery implanting one of the many varied types of chips, the Servitor will automatically join your colony as a functional pawn. Each chip determines the Servitor’s designated role and may restrict other capabilities, such as combat, skilled labor, or thinking.

Included are numerous implants designed to enhance and maintain your Servitors throughout their long-term service to the colony. Keep in mind that Servitors are intended to be expendable resources. Over time, their biological components will deteriorate under constant strain, making implant recovery and bodily recycling an important part of maintaining efficiency.

Servitors are also generally slower and less efficient than regular pawns in certain tasks - such as walking. Take good care of your decaying little helpers!

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/SERV/Contents_02.png[/img]

Combat Servitors can also benefit from replacing their arms with integrated weapon systems for sustained firepower. While Melta weaponry is included in the mod, the current version does not provide access to Heavy Bolter or Plasma arm variants unless [b]RimDark’s [i][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3505068525]Mankind’s Finest[/url][/i][/b] or [b]Segmentum’s [i][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3654547350]Children of the Omnissiah[/url][/i][/b] are installed, respectively.

Servitors are cybernetically augmented thralls created through the lobotomization and mechanical conversion of humans, designed to perform a vast range of tasks across the Imperium of Mankind. While most are used as labor units for physically demanding and repetitive work, Servitors also fulfill logistical, medical, and combat-oriented roles. As such, a closer use of these, in your RimWorld would be/look like:
- Prisoners, slaves, and criminals being converted into Servitors;
- Heavy labor such as agriculture, hauling, and mining delegated to Servitors;
- Combat Servitors deployed to reinforce your military forces as expendable frontline units or decoys;
- Auxiliary Servitors assigned to basic medical assistance, maintenance, or research support tasks.

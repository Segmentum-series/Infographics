[b]Warrant of the Void[/b] is a mod focused on adding Rogue Traders to your RimWorld. Serve under a Rogue Trader, an insanely wealthy noble with an ancient warrant of the void. They are exploring the uncharted Segmentum Mundus Marginalis, and making sure their extreme wealth and power are known to everyone.

[b][i]This mod was commissioned by Lord of Pancakes and Muffins.[/i][/b]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3654537135]Follow our collection for more "[b]Warhammer 40,000[/b]" content[/url], by REDACTED.

All content is subject to change as we continue to review and improve the mod. Additional features and refinements will be introduced over time. This is a fan-made project developed by fans, in our available spare time, and we appreciate your understanding and continued support as the mod evolves.

- [i]Abomination, REDACTED's current Chairman[/i]

[b]Q:[/b] Do these spawn on enemies?
[b]A:[/b] Only on the mod's added faction.

[b]Q:[/b] How much integration is there with Save Our Ship 2 and Odyssey?
[b]A:[/b] There are specific starting scenarios for both SOS2 and Odyssey, and when playing SOS2 the added Imperium pirate faction has its own unique ship designs for space combat. Odyssey enables the mining laser weapon/tool for use, also.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod safe to be added mid-save game?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, it should be safe to add, but removing could cause issues to your save.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod going to receive updates in the future OR be maintained by the authors?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, we plan to expand the mod in the future. You can keep track of our plans in our [url=https://discord.gg/UtXexRemce][b]Discord server[/b][/url].

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod CE compatible?
[b]A:[/b] Currently not.

[b]Q:[/b] Is there a Discord server?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, [url=https://discord.gg/UtXexRemce][b]Discord server[/b][/url].

[b]Q:[/b] Can I suggest something OR share my ideas, for this or other future mods in the Segmentum series?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, join our [url=https://discord.gg/UtXexRemce][b]Discord server[/b][/url], and send your suggestions in the #ideas channel].

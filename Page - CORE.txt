[b]Core[/b] is dedicated to hosting dependency files used by the [b][i]"Segmentum"[/i][/b] mods series for RimWorld. It contains essential Segmentum code, and should therefore not be used unless other Segmentum mods are enabled in your playthrough.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/Generic/Gif.gif[/img]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3654537135]Follow our collection for more "[b]Warhammer 40,000[/b]" content[/url], by REDACTED.

Content is subject to change as we continue to review and expand the mod’s dependency resources. Additional features and refinements will be introduced over time. This is a fan-made project developed in our available spare time, and we appreciate your understanding and continued support as the series evolves.

This mod serves as a host for dependency files used by the Segmentum series. When enabled, it provides access to select shared content required by other modules, and a handful of new additions. Non-dependency when enabled include:
[list]
[*][b][i]TwoLargeGayMen's[/i] "Imperial Date System"[/b] - Modifying the standard year system of RimWorld, to display a Warhammer 40,000 stylized year system. You are able to pick a variety of years as well as fully customize the displayed date, just remember to save and reload your game for it to be displayed.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/CORE/Contents_01.png[/img]

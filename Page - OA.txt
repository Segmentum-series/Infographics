[b]Officio Assassinorum[/b] is a mod focused on adding Imperial Assassins to your RimWorld. Divided in temples dedicated to special techniques of elimination, these elite assassins are a nigh-insurmountable threat even to the most fearsome and dangerous enemies of the Imperium of Man.

[b][i]This mod was commissioned by MDRay01.[/i][/b]

[b]Q:[/b] Do these spawn on enemies?
[b]A:[/b] No.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod safe to be added mid-save game?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, it should be safe to add, but removing could cause issues to your save.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod going to receive updates in the future OR be maintained by the authors?

[b]Children of the Omnissiah[/b] is a mod focused on depicting the Adeptus Mechanicus within the [b][i]"Segmentum series"[/i][/b] for RimWorld. The Adeptus Mechanicus, also known as the Priesthood of Mars, is a technological order that holds a monopoly  over the Imperium of Mankind’s technological knowledge and industrial doctrine.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/COTO/Contents_01.png[/img]

Weapons require at minimum two researches to become craftable. [b]Keep in mind the warnings listed below,[/b] as these weapons can be as dangerous and lethal to the wielder as they are to their targets.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/COTO/Contents_02.png[/img]

Additionally, [b]Radiation poisoning[/b] can be treated with a neutroamine infusion, available in the "Operations tab" of your pawn.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Segmentum-series/Infographics/refs/heads/main/COTO/Contents_03.png[/img]

[b]Sicarians[/b] are an optional feature, meaning that you'll just be unable to see or use them if [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2925432336][b]Big & Small Framework[/b][/url] is not enabled.

[b]Q:[/b] Do these weapons spawn on enemies?
[b]A:[/b] The weapons will only be used by the hostile Explorators faction added by the mod. Random raiders and caravaneers will not use them.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod safe to be added mid-save game?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, it should be safe to add, but removing may cause massive issues.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod going to receive updates in the future OR be maintained by the authors?
[b]A:[/b] Yes. You can keep track of our plans in our [url=https://discord.gg/UtXexRemce][b]Discord server[/b][/url].

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod CE compatible?
[b]A:[/b] Currently not, but we do not handle Combat Extended's patching, feel free to contact CE's team for that!

[b]Q:[/b] Can I suggest something OR share my ideas?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, join our [url=https://discord.gg/UtXexRemce][b]Discord server[/b][/url], and send your suggestions in the #ideas channel].

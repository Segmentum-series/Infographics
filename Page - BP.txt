[b]Sabbat's Blight[/b] is a mod focused on adding the Blood Pact to your RimWorld. Serve to Khorne, the Blood God. This mod is part of our very special collaboration with REDACTED ([url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3654537135]from the Segmentum series[/url].).

[b][i]This mod was commissioned by BruceTheRonin.[/i][/b]

Keep in mind that any content added can be subjected to changes in the future.

[b]Q:[/b] Do these spawn on enemies?
[b]A:[/b] Only on the mod's added faction.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod safe to be added mid-save game?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, it should be safe to add, but removing could cause issues to your save.

[b]Q:[/b] Is this mod CE compatible?
[b]A:[/b] Currently not.
